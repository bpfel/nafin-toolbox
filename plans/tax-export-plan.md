# Tax Export Feature Idea

Saved on 2026-02-07. Not yet planned in detail.

## Concept
Direct export of relevant financial data for Swiss tax filing. The app already tracks income, expenses, retirement contributions (3a), and investments — all of which feed into the annual tax declaration. A dedicated export could pull the right numbers together automatically.

## Potential Scope
- Annual summary of income and deductions
- Pillar 3a contributions (deductible up to legal cap)
- Investment holdings and valuations (wealth tax)
- Account balances as of Dec 31 (wealth tax)
- Output format TBD (PDF, CSV, or structured data matching cantonal tax software import)
